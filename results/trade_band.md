# Trade-band test at IBKR, after French tax

Pre-registered 2026-11-26 **before** running. Baseline: TECH monthly, 2.5x,
SMH core, HIFO, French CTO at Interactive Brokers (2 bps FX), 5 bps spread ->
**57.01x after tax**, maxDD -67.8%, 2018 -17.3%, 2022 -56.6%, 1,836 trades,
€135k PFU paid.

Hypothesis: skipping monthly rebalance trades whose target drift is below a
band cuts turnover, PFU and fees without losing the sleeve signal.

Variants: `trade_band` = 0 (baseline), 2.5%, 5%, 10%, 20% of NAV.

Acceptance: after-tax terminal > **57.01x**, maxDD not worse by more than 2 pp,
and **both** 2018 and 2022 not worse than monthly. If a band passes, check
neighbors and all-year returns before adoption; a single-point spike is rejected.

Run: `uv run python scripts/trade_band.py`.

## Decision: keep 0 for the shipped/forward book

The pre-registered plateau, annual and bootstrap checks all pass, but the gain
is **+0.84x (+1.5%)**, annual differences are within ±1.1 pp, and bootstrap p5
moves only +0.04x. That is inside single-path noise. The monthly book is
currently the forward paper book with 1/5 dates; changing its config for a
marginal backtest gain would reset that validation for no proven live benefit.

`trade_band=0.025` is available as an opt-in (`Config.trade_band` / custom run)
after the forward gate passes. Default stays 0.
