# Sleeve breadth after French tax at IBKR

Pre-registered 2026-11-26 **before** running. Baseline: TECH monthly, 6-name
sleeve, SMH core, 2.5x, HIFO, French CTO at IBKR (2 bps FX), 5 bps spread ->
**57.01x after tax**, maxDD -67.8%, 2018 -17.3%, 2022 -56.6%.

Hypothesis: under IBKR's low FX and 31.4% PFU, a different number of sleeve names
may improve after-tax return without a worse tail. Test 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10.

Acceptance: after-tax > **57.01x**, maxDD not worse by more than 2 pp, both
2018 and 2022 not worse, and bootstrap p5 not worse. A single-name-count spike
is rejected; neighbors must cluster.

Run: `uv run python scripts/topn_fr.py`.

## Near-pass note: 4 names

4 names is the only serious alternative: **75.27x after tax**, maxDD -68.9%,
2018 -13.6% (better), 2022 -56.8% (**0.2 pp worse**), bootstrap p5 3.27x
(better than 6's 2.85x). It fails the strict "2022 not worse" rule by 0.2 pp,
and it doubles single-name concentration (4 names at 2.5x). The 5-name neighbor
drops to 53.36x, so the curve is not a clean plateau.

Keep 6 for the forward book. 4 is a risk-on candidate only after a start-date
audit and a deliberate reset of the 1/5 forward history.
