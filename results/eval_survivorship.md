# Survivorship audit: TECH static-49 and the US point-in-time book

- Data snapshot: **2026-11-25**
- TECH universe: **49 static names**, chosen today and applied as a single 2014-01-01 membership snapshot.
- At the 2015 start, **28/49** were S&P 500 members; **15** joined later; **6** were never in the S&P 500.
- **8** names have no price at the 2015 start (later IPO or missing data); **0** have no usable price at all.
- US book: **128/789** unique 2014+ S&P members (**16.2%**) have no usable price and are silently excluded. That is the US book's residual survivorship bias.

## What the data actually is

- TECH membership source: the hand-written list in `bot/markets.py`, one snapshot at `2014-01-01`. It is not a historical index constituent file.
- US membership source: the fja05680 S&P 500 history CSV (`bot/universe.py`), point-in-time, plus four manual 2026 changes.
- Missing Yahoo histories: **421** symbols in `cache/missing_symbols.txt` (delisted, acquired or renamed); the US panel drops them. That is residual survivorship bias even in the US book.

Static equal-weight basket: **19.46x** over the window vs QQQ **6.71x**; median name 7.85x; best NVDA 309.83x, worst ANT 0.85x. This mixes selection, equal weighting and the 2026 list; it is evidence of a premium, not a clean estimate.

## What claims are safe

- SAFE: The monthly rotation logic is reproducible on the committed cache.
- SAFE: The after-tax multiple is an upper bound with today's winners in the universe.
- SAFE: ETF-only benchmarks (QQQ, SMH) are survivorship-free at the fund level.
- UNSAFE: That the TECH stock sleeve's alpha is investable without a point-in-time universe.
- UNSAFE: That the 57x after-tax multiple is an expected forward return.
- UNSAFE: That the static-49 momentum result generalises to the NASDAQ universe of 2015.

**Bottom line:** the 57.01x TECH headline is an upper bound on a biased universe, not an expected return. Report ETF-only (QQQ/SMH) results as survivorship-free evidence; treat stock-sleeve alpha as unproven until a point-in-time NASDAQ membership file with delisted names is added.
