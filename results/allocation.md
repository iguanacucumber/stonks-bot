## Reading

- 100% bot maximizes money: 57.5% CAGR, but a -61% drawdown.
- 60% bot / 40% SPY maximizes Sharpe (1.05) at 42.7% CAGR and a -43% drawdown.
- The low correlation (0.66) is why: the bot is not just levered SPY.
- Taxes are ignored here; the taxable variant of the bot (`--profile tax`) has
  lower turnover and should be used in a taxable account before blending.
- Monthly rebalancing of the 50/50 blend gives 38.2% CAGR, Sharpe 1.05, maxDD -41.6%: the result is not a daily-rebalance artifact.
