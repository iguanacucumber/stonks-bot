# Leverage after French tax at IBKR

Pre-registered 2026-11-26 **before** running. Baseline: TECH monthly, SMH core,
2.5x, 500 bps financing, HIFO, French CTO at IBKR (2 bps FX), 5 bps spread ->
**57.01x after tax**, maxDD -67.8%, 2018 -17.3%, 2022 -56.6%.

Hypothesis: after 31.4% PFU and 500 bps financing, the after-tax optimum may not
be 2.5x. Test 1.5x, 2.0x, 2.5x, 3.0x.

Acceptance for a new default: after-tax terminal > **57.01x**, maxDD not worse by
more than 5 pp, both 2018 and 2022 not worse, and bootstrap p5 not worse.
Otherwise keep 2.5x and document the trade-off.

Run: `uv run python scripts/leverage_fr.py`.
