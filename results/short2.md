## Verdict

- pairs OOS beats QQQ after tax: **FAIL**
- pairs OOS beats BIL (1.20x headline): **PASS**
- pairs survives 2x costs: **PASS**
- event 20d placebo p<0.05: **FAIL**
- intraday sample >20 trades: **FAIL**

**Verdict: `reject`.** Pairs are the closest family, but the honest OOS path is below QQQ after tax and the fixed full-window edge is one sector pair, SMH/SOXX, not a broad relative-value premium. The 8-K drift is indistinguishable from the date-shuffle placebo, and the intraday sample is far too small to be evidence. No satellite allocation; keep the monthly book.
